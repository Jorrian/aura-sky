Support

Aura Sky is a calm Apple-native weather app for everyday awareness across Mac, iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, and Apple Vision Pro.

Supported platforms

iOS 26+, iPadOS 26+, macOS 26+, watchOS 26+, and visionOS 26+.

Core features

Clean forecasts, radar, daily insights, optional Mood Rhythm, widgets, watch glances, and Aura Pro upgrades.

Privacy posture

No account is required. Location is optional and controlled through Apple system permissions.

Data sources

Aura Sky uses Apple Weather and may use other public or third-party weather data providers.

Important disclaimer

Aura Sky is provided for informational purposes only. It is not certified, validated, endorsed, or approved by any authority and must not be used as the primary source for flight planning, navigation, weather briefing, dispatch, or operational decision-making.

Always rely on official, approved sources for safety-critical use. Weather data may vary in accuracy, completeness, timeliness, and availability.

Apple Weather attribution is shown directly in the app and links to the WeatherKit legal attribution page.
