Privacy Policy

Effective April 3, 2026

Aura Sky respects your privacy.

Information Aura Sky collects

Aura Sky does not require a user account for core app functionality and does not collect directly identifying personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number for basic use of the app.

Information processed on device

Depending on the features you use, Aura Sky may process or store information locally on your device, such as selected locations or saved places, app settings and appearance preferences, app usage state needed for the user experience, and approximate location if you choose to enable Location Services.

Location Services

If you enable Location Services, Aura Sky may use your device’s approximate location to support relevant features, such as showing locally relevant weather. Location access is governed by system permissions and can be changed in your device settings at any time.

Third-party data retrieval

Aura Sky may retrieve weather or related informational content from third-party or public sources in order to provide app functionality. Aura Sky does not sell personal information and is not a social network or advertising resale platform.

No sale of personal data

Aura Sky does not sell, rent, or broker your personal information.

Data retention

Any locally stored preferences or app state remain subject to your device, system settings, backups, and any Apple platform services you choose to enable.

Your choices

You may disable Location Services for Aura Sky at any time in system settings. You may also remove the app and its locally stored data from your devices at any time, subject to backup or sync behavior outside the developer’s control.

Policy updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of Aura Sky after an update means the revised policy applies from its effective date.

Support

Support information for Aura Sky is available through the official support page.
