The OS 27 update

A fresh outlook.
The same calm weather.

We’re bringing Aura Sky to iOS 27, iPadOS 27, macOS 27, watchOS 27, and visionOS 27, with thoughtful updates to your everyday weather experience.

  • A focused forecast briefing to help plan your next few hours.
  • Saved places in Spotlight and your weather shortcuts.
  • Simpler Mood check-ins with gentle, mood-driven ambience.

See the App Store for the version currently available on your device. Screenshots show the Aura Sky experience; appearance and features can vary by version and platform.

Weather that stays useful.

Aura Sky keeps the main surface focused on practical weather, radar, and daily context, then layers in personal rhythm only when it helps.

Clean forecasts

Current conditions, hourly forecasts, daily forecasts, and feels-like temperature in a clear layout.

Real-time radar

Track rain and changing conditions with a clear radar map. Aura Pro unlocks playback and premium layers.

Today's Insight

Get a simple summary of the day’s weather so you can plan with less effort.

Mood Rhythm

Optionally log how you feel and see patterns connected to your environment.

Aura Pro adds deeper weather tools.

  • Personal comfort settings, smarter notifications, and iCloud Sync.

Support and legal information.

Aura Sky is designed for everyday weather awareness. It is not a certified source for aviation, dispatch, navigation, safety-critical weather briefing, or operational decision-making.
